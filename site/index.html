01 · The loop

Build. Absorb. Ascend.

Everything runs through Create machines, so the only limit on your power is the size of your factory.

  1. Mine foreverCobble generators feed mixers that make mineral clusters. Crush them for every ore, even diamonds and netherite.
  2. Assemble coresA Create assembly line turns diamonds, rose quartz and lava into Ascension Cores.
  3. Absorb EssenceRight-click cores into your Essence bank, or melt them into Liquid Essence and pipe it to Altars and Condensers.
  4. Unlock enchantsMix a tome, hold it next to any gear and right-click. Any enchant goes on any item.
  5. AscendSpend Essence on levels with no cap. Then go further out, where the mobs and the loot get scarier.

03 · Mythic

✦ The rare, crazy tier

Mythic tomes can't be made from factory materials alone. They need Mythic Fragments, which only drop from mobs out in the danger zones, mostly from Elites. The factory makes you strong. Exploring makes you strange.

04 · Ultimates

☄ Sneak. Hit. Unleash.

Ultimates are cinematic combo moves. Sneak and hit an enemy, and the game takes over: you blink around it, launch into the sky, call down lightning, and land right back where you started. You can't be hurt mid-combo, and every hit scales with your other enchants. Their tomes need Ascended Souls, which only Ascended bosses drop.

07 · Safety

Insane power. Zero dupes.

Uncapped numbers are only fun if they can't break your world. Every script is checked outside the game before it ships.

No duplication

  • Loot bonuses never copy gear or items with NBT, so a mob can't duplicate your sword.
  • Upgrades are all-or-nothing, with rollback. Tomes are only used up when an unlock succeeds.
  • A fuzz test runs 3,000 random moves and checks that every bit of Essence is accounted for.

No corruption

  • Essence has a backup copy in the player's file, and it restores itself if world data is lost.
  • NaN, negative or out-of-range values read as 0 and are never written.
  • A startup config check switches off anything broken instead of crashing.

No runaway effects

  • Chain effects can't chain into each other, so a Meteor kill can't blow up a whole farm.
  • Area damage only hits hostile mobs. Lightning is visual only.
  • /kill and the void always work, whatever your Protection.
