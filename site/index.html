01 · The loop

Mine. Build. Absorb. Ascend.

It starts with a glowing ore and a rock hammer. It ends with a factory that turns cobblestone into godhood.

  1. Find AscenditeA glowing ore between iron and diamond. Smash rock with a Rock Hammer, or press 9 cobblestone into Raw Ascendite.
  2. Mine foreverCobble generators feed mixers that make mineral clusters. Crush them for every ore, even diamonds and netherite.
  3. Assemble coresAssembly lines, presses or a crafting table turn your ores into Ascension Cores. Absorb them, or melt them into Liquid Essence.
  4. Pull tomesCraft tomes, rip open card packs, crack Gear Caches. Hold a tome next to any gear and right-click.
  5. AscendSpend Essence on levels with no cap, then go further out, where the mobs and the loot get scarier.

02 · Luck

Every chest is a lottery.

Enchantment Packs turn up in dungeon chests, temples, strongholds and End cities. Five tome cards each, the last one Rare or better, and any card can come foiled. This opens packs with the game's own odds.

 

Foils

A foil multiplies the tome's level when you apply it. Pack cards roll them (better cards, better foils), 15% of loot tomes come foiled, and you can craft Foil Sheets and press them onto any tome.

Sealed Gear Caches

One piece of relic gear in a sealed crate. It turns up in vanilla chests, and what's inside depends on where you open it: carry it out to the danger zones first.

Jackpots

Card and relic levels have a long lucky tail. Most rolls are normal. Some are not.

    And of vanilla chests hide a jackpot stack: up to 40 packs, 16 caches, 6 booster boxes or 192 cores.

    Rare mobs

    Every mob that spawns rolls once. Kills are announced to the whole server.

    03 · Gear

    Four new metals. No two swords alike.

    Ascendite takes you past iron. Voidsteel, Celestium and Ascendium take you past netherite. Then condition, attachments and Artifacts make each piece yours.

    Condition

    Every piece of gear rolls a wear grade once, like a weapon skin. Pristine hits 25% harder. A Polishing Cloth rerolls it and keeps the better grade.

     

    Attachments

    Two slots on every piece. Hold an attachment next to the gear and right-click. Hover one to see what it does.

     

    One of one

    Artifacts

    Push a piece of gear past total enchant levels and Awaken it. It gets a name, a world serial number, a signature color and aura, a lore line and 3 to 4 quirks with rolled strengths. There will never be another.

    04 · Enchants

    486 enchants. No ceiling.

    Drag the level, pick a foil, and watch them scale. These numbers come straight from the pack's config, so they're exactly what you get in game.

    05 · Mythic

    ✦ The rare, crazy tier

    Mythic tomes can't be made from factory materials alone. They need Mythic Fragments, which only drop from mobs out in the danger zones, mostly from Elites. The factory makes you strong. Exploring makes you strange.

    06 · Ultimates

    ☄ Sneak. Hit. Unleash.

    Ultimates are cinematic combo moves. Sneak and hit an enemy, and the game takes over: you blink around it, launch into the sky, call down lightning, and land right back where you started. You can't be hurt mid-combo, and every hit scales with your other enchants. Their tomes need Ascended Souls, which only Ascended bosses drop. Or a Secret Rare pull.

    11 · Safety

    Insane power. Zero dupes.

    Uncapped numbers are only fun if they can't break your world. Every script is checked outside the game, and again inside a real server, before it ships.

    No duplication

    • Loot bonuses never copy gear or items with NBT, so a mob can't duplicate your sword.
    • Upgrades are all-or-nothing, with rollback. Tomes are only used up when an unlock succeeds.
    • A fuzz test runs 3,000 random moves and checks that every bit of Essence is accounted for.

    No corruption

    • Essence has a backup copy in the player's file, and it restores itself if world data is lost.
    • NaN, negative or out-of-range values read as 0 and are never written.
    • A startup config check switches off anything broken instead of crashing.

    Tested in game

    • /ascension selftest builds a test spot far from players and runs 55 real checks (60 with the Full mod), then cleans up.
    • Real right-clicks with a fake player: tomes, foils, packs, hammers, the altar and every machine.
    • /kill and the void always work, whatever your Protection.
